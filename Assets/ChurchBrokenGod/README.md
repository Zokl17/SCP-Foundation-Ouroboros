# Church of the Broken God art sources

The four PNGs in this directory are the original transparent outputs from the built-in imagegen tool. No reference illustration from the user or the SCP wiki was copied into the mod. The vestment, mask and guard-armor atlases are sliced into south, north, west and east worn textures under `Textures/Things/Pawn/Humanlike/Apparel/`. The icon source is used at `Textures/World/FactionIcons/BrokenGod.png`.

The visual reference was the traditional Broken Church in the [Church of the Broken God hub](https://scpfoundation.net/church-of-the-broken-god-hub): pale ceremonial clothing, brass clockwork and visible augmentation. The source distinguishes the traditional branch from the Cogwork Orthodox Church and Maxwellism; these assets represent the traditional branch in the SCP-882 raid.

Imagegen prompts:

1. `Create an ORIGINAL production game asset: a 4-direction worn-apparel sprite atlas for a top-down 2D colony-sim character (RimWorld-like readability, not copying any existing game art). Asset is an off-white ecclesiastical clockwork robe for a fictional Church of the Broken God. Shape: ONLY the clothing worn on torso, shoulders, arms and lower body, hollow/transparent where the human head and face would be; ivory layered vestment, graphite under-robe, narrow antique brass trim and rivets, modest gear-shaped emblem over the chest, asymmetric mechanical shoulder pauldron. Flat hand-painted pixel-art with sharp edges and small clear details, no lighting effects, no cast shadow. Four equal isolated views in a precise 2x2 grid: upper left front/south, upper right back/north, lower left side/east, lower right side/west. Each view centered in its quadrant, same scale and identical silhouette proportions appropriate for a 128x128 RimWorld worn-apparel texture. Each quadrant has generous transparent padding; no overlap or touching cell borders. NO body, NO skin, NO head, NO face, NO mask, NO text, NO labels, NO background, NO frame, NO extra object. Truly transparent RGBA background.`
2. `Create an ORIGINAL production game asset: four-view worn headgear sprite atlas for a top-down 2D colony-sim character, same clean hand-painted pixel-art style and proportions as the previously generated ivory/brass clockwork vestment in this conversation. A Church of the Broken God acolyte's distinctive mask and hood: off-white cloth hood, dark bronze and brass riveted mechanical half-face mask, a single cog-like brass ocular rim and small exposed clockwork pieces, solemn occult-industrial priest aesthetic. It should read clearly at RimWorld pawn size without looking like a modern military helmet. Four equal isolated views in precise 2x2 grid: upper left front/south, upper right back/north, lower left side/east, lower right side/west. Same scale in every quadrant, generous transparent padding, each sprite centered for a 128x128 worn apparel texture. Headgear ONLY, no human head, no skin, no torso or robe, no text, no labels, no props, no shadow, no background, no frame. True transparent RGBA background.`
3. `Original faction emblem for a fictional clockwork religious order, the Church of the Broken God. Single bold antique-brass icon on a genuinely transparent background, centered square composition. A thick circular gear ring visibly split in one place, with a small simple hammer silhouette crossing a broken cog at the center; geometric, legible at 32 pixels, mostly solid shapes with a few deep charcoal cutouts. Inspired by industrial sacred heraldry but do NOT copy any existing SCP logo or supplied reference artwork. Flat 2D vector-style symbol only, no text, no gradients, no bevel, no shadows, no border, no background.`
4. `Original production sprite atlas for a 2D top-down colony-sim game. The Church of the Broken God's armored reliquary guard, visually related to the ivory-and-brass clockwork vestment and hooded mask previously generated in this conversation. WORN BODY APPAREL ONLY, no head, no helmet, no face, no body underneath. A stout enclosed cuirass made from aged dark bronze gear plates, rivets, articulated shoulder shells, ivory ceremonial tabard at center, hints of clockwork at the waist, practical armored skirt. Clear heavier combat silhouette than the earlier cloth vestment but same faction palette. Crisp hand-painted pixel art with transparent background, no shadow or glow. Four equal isolated views in an exact 2x2 grid: upper-left front/south, upper-right back/north, lower-left side facing left, lower-right side facing right. Center each on an equal transparent quadrant with generous padding; consistent scale and silhouette for worn body armor sprites after downscaling to 256x256. No text, labels, background, frame, weapons or extra object.`

In the generated atlases, the lower-left subject actually faces west and lower-right faces east. The original exports followed the visual direction. Current worn sprites use the east art for both side texture files because RimWorld mirrors the west view at draw time. Body-type fitting and the corrected reliquary-guard back source are documented in `Art/Source/ApparelFit_20261028/README.md`.
